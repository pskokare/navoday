Conditions Evaluated

Conditions Dr. Tupere Evaluates

Consult for anorectal symptoms that need examination and a condition-specific treatment discussion.

Piles (Haemorrhoids)

If you are looking for a piles doctor in Kharadi, Pune, Dr. Tupere can assess symptoms such as bleeding, discomfort or a lump. The type and severity of piles help determine whether non-surgical care, an office procedure or surgery should be discussed.

Anal Fissure

An anal fissure can cause pain during or after a bowel movement, sometimes with bleeding. Dr. Tupere evaluates new and persistent symptoms and explains treatment options after confirming the diagnosis.

Anal Fistula

An anal fistula may cause recurrent swelling or discharge near the anus. Dr. Tupere evaluates its location and complexity before discussing a suitable treatment approach.

Pilonidal Sinus

A pilonidal sinus can cause pain, swelling or discharge near the top of the buttock cleft. Dr. Tupere assesses new, infected or recurrent concerns and discusses the next steps according to the examination findings.
