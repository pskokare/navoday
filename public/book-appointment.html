How It Works

From Appointment Request to a Clear Care Plan

We keep the process straightforward. Send a request, speak with Dr. Tupere about your symptoms and review the next steps based on your consultation and examination.

01

Tell Us Your Concern

Choose the reason for your visit, or select “Not Sure” if you do not know what is causing your symptoms.

02

Confirm Your Appointment

Share a preferred date. Our team will contact you to confirm an available time and answer practical booking questions.

03

Discuss Your Symptoms

Tell Dr. Tupere what you have noticed, how long it has been happening and whether you have had treatment before.

04

Clinical Assessment

Your medical history and an examination, where appropriate, help identify the condition and guide the discussion.

05

Understand Your Options

Dr. Tupere explains the findings and discusses suitable care options for your condition.

06

Prepare for Follow-Up

If treatment or another visit is advised, the clinic explains preparation, scheduling and follow-up.

Your care plan depends on the findings during your consultation and examination.
